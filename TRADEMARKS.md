# Apelora marks — draft brand-use policy

**APELORA**, **Apelora Fabric**, **Apelora Studio**, and any official project logo are intended brand identifiers. Trademark registration and ownership are **not represented as complete**.

The AGPLv3 code license does not itself grant permission to represent an unrelated product as official Apelora software or falsely imply affiliation, certification or endorsement.

Nominative, accurate attribution of compatibility and other uses allowed by applicable law remain unaffected. Forks should use clearly distinct branding and must retain copyright and license notices required by the applicable license.

Use of logos in a way implying official endorsement requires written permission from the trademark owner, where legally enforceable. Community-use guidelines and a formal trademark policy should be reviewed before the project grows.

Third-party product logos in appliance catalogs remain trademarks of their respective owners and require their own usage review.
